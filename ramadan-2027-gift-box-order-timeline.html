Ramadan 2027 gift boxes: plan from your arrival date.

Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around February 8, just two days after Chinese New Year on February 6. Custom Ramadan packaging made in China therefore has to leave the factory before the pre-holiday slowdown.

Custom paper boxes: MOQ 1,000 per design; samples about 7 working days; bulk 25–35 working days after sample approval. Freight is separate. Other components and complex formats need a project-specific quote. Company and ordering facts.

Short answer

Two holidays, one deadline.

Use the earlier of two dates: the supplier and forwarder’s confirmed last pickup date, or your required warehouse receipt date minus the complete delivery window and buffer. Then work backward through QC, production, materials, sample courier, review and artwork.

January dispatch does not guarantee arrival before Ramadan. A longer freight route may require December dispatch; confirm transit, customs, final delivery and receiving time before approving a production schedule.

Boundary: Muhammadiyah’s 2027 calendar places the first day of Ramadan on February 8. Treat it as a planning reference; confirm Ramadan and Eid observance locally. A religious calendar does not confirm factory or transport availability.

Buyer questions

Ramadan 2027 packaging FAQ.

Holiday dates are expected dates; confirm production and freight dates in writing.

February 8, 2027 is the first day in Muhammadiyah’s published calendar. Confirm local Ramadan and Eid dates with the relevant authority before setting a retail launch or delivery deadline.
Chinese New Year falls on February 6, 2027, close to the expected start of Ramadan. Confirm factory closure and freight cutoffs separately; the delivery route may require dispatch earlier than January.
Use the earlier of two dates: the supplier and forwarder’s confirmed last pickup date, or your required warehouse receipt date minus the complete delivery window and buffer. Then work backward through QC, production, materials, sample courier, review and artwork.
The published MOQ for custom paper boxes is 1,000 pieces per design. Confirm complex calendar formats, paper bags and other components individually. Combining items in one collection does not combine their minimum quantities.
It is possible but risky: production restarts gradually after the holiday, and freight still has to reach the Middle East before Eid. Plan Ramadan and Eid packaging in one pre-holiday order where you can.

Check the calendar and the commercial dates separately.

Working days are not calendar days. Convert each quoted stage using the responsible supplier’s working calendar and closure dates before entering it in the lead time planner.
