One accountable workflow

Coordinate the system without pretending every material is identical.

Boxes, bags, and labels can share the same brand references, but substrates and print methods produce color, gloss, and texture differently. We approve each component in its real construction, then review how the set works together.

  • One product and packing brief across structural components
  • Material-specific artwork and color approval
  • Physical fit and opening sequence reviewed before production
  • Master-carton and delivery planning based on the complete set
