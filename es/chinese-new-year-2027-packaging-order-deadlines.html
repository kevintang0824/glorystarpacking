Chinese New Year 2027: plan packaging orders Al revés.

Chinese New Year 2027 falls on Saturday, February 6. Custom packaging that must ship before the holiday needs final artwork, an approved sample and booked materials well before factories slow down.

Ship before the slowdown, not before the holiday date.

Chinese New Year 2027 is on February 6, 2027. Packaging factories typically slow down in the weeks before the holiday as staff travel home, close for the holiday period, and return to full capacity gradually afterwards. The date that matters for your order is therefore the supplier's last pickup day, not February 6.

Ask every supplier, in writing, for four dates: last production day, last pickup day, reopening day and expected full-capacity day. Then plan your artwork, sample and production backward from the last pickup day.

Boundary: mainland China announces its official holiday arrangement separately, and each factory sets its own closure. This guide gives the planning method; confirm actual dates with your supplier and forwarder.

1 · Work backward

The pre-holiday packaging timeline.

Each milestone has to be finished before the next can start. Replace the durations with the lead times your supplier quotes for your exact box, finish and quantity.

For a new custom rigid or magnetic box, this chain usually means artwork and dieline should be final by the end of November 2026, so that sampling, approval and bulk production can finish before the January slowdown. Use the lead time planner to calculate your own dates from the quoted durations.

2 · Reduce holiday risk

Five ways to protect a launch around Chinese New Year.

  • Freeze artwork earlyLate text, barcode or colour changes are the most common reason a sample has to be remade.
  • Approve one physical sampleLock board, wrap, finish, insert and colour target on a signed sample before the factory slows down.
  • Book materials with the orderSpecialty papers, magnets, ribbons and foils can sell out before the holiday; confirm they are reserved.
  • Split the order if neededShip a smaller pre-holiday batch for launch and schedule the balance for after full-capacity restart.
  • Hold buffer stockCover the closure plus the slow restart weeks, especially for packaging tied to Valentine's Day or spring launches.

Agree the delivery basis early too: see the EXW, FOB, CIF and DDP guide for who books freight in each case.

3 · Send this now

What to send your supplier this week.

A complete brief lets the supplier confirm whether your order can still ship before the holiday.

    Product and box details

    Box style, internal size, product weight, insert, quantity per SKU and number of SKUs.

  2. 02

    Final or draft artwork, dieline status, Pantone targets, foil and embossing areas.

  3. 03

    Date the packaging must arrive, destination and preferred delivery basis.

  4. 04

    Last production, last pickup, reopening and full-capacity dates, confirmed in writing. Use the Plantilla RFQ to keep quotes comparable.

Preguntas del comprador

Chinese New Year packaging FAQ.

Closure dates vary by factory and region; always confirm them with your supplier.

Chinese New Year 2027 falls on Saturday, February 6, 2027. Mainland China publishes its official public-holiday arrangement separately, so confirm the dates each supplier and logistics provider will actually close and reopen.
There is no single closure length. Production usually slows before the holiday as workers travel home and recovers gradually afterwards. Ask each supplier in writing for its last production day, last pickup day, reopening date and expected full-capacity date.
Work backward from the supplier’s confirmed last pickup date: subtract bulk production, quality inspection and packing time, then sample approval, then artwork and dieline approval. For new custom boxes, artwork usually needs to be final well before the end of November 2026.
Plan for no sample or production activity during the closure. Approve the pre-production sample, colour target and packing specification before the last working day so bulk work can start immediately on return.
Agree a written post-holiday start and ship date, lock materials and artwork now, consider splitting the order into a smaller pre-holiday batch and a later balance, and hold buffer stock for the weeks when supply is slow to recover.
