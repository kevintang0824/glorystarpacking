What this reference shows

A visual direction—not a stock promise.

The image is useful for discussing opening action, interior artwork, product presentation, and color coverage. Engineering begins only after the real contents and route are known.

01 · Structure

Roll-end opening experience

The reference shows a hinged corrugated mailer with side walls and a front tuck. Locking geometry, flute, scores, and panel depth must be redrawn to suit the confirmed product and packed load.

02 · Print

Interior and exterior artwork

Broad color fields and illustrated interior panels can be reviewed together. The chosen liner, ink process, small type, folds, and rub exposure determine the final prepress and proofing route.

03 · Pack-out

Presentation with movement control

The shown tissue and sample products are styling references only. A real pack-out may need a fitted insert, paper support, divider, or another movement-control method based on fragility and the parcel journey.

Specification checklist

Confirm the decisions the image cannot answer.

A useful quotation requires more than a preferred appearance. Send the packed product data and commercial constraints so construction, print, sample scope, and logistics can be evaluated together.

Product & fit
Product or internal dimensions, packed weight, fragility, orientation, and acceptable movement.
Transit route
Destination, parcel or pallet route, handling risks, return needs, and any test standard owned by your project.
Artwork & finish
Print coverage, color references, liner preference, finish direction, barcodes, labels, and areas exposed to rub.
Commercial brief
Estimated quantity, target in-hand date, packing method, flat-storage limits, and delivery location.

Approval route

Approve fit before final artwork.

A structural sample answers whether the box folds, locks, protects, and presents the actual contents. Once that geometry is accepted, a printed proof can be used to review color, registration, folds, and opening sequence on the intended material.

  • Check internal clearance and product movement with the real pack-out
  • Review assembly time and closure security at the packing bench
  • Keep critical artwork away from scores, locks, and hidden panels
  • Confirm export packing and delivery assumptions before production
