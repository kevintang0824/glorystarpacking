Réponse courte

Two holidays, one deadline.

Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin on or around February 8, 2027, and Eid al-Fitr around March 9–10. Chinese New Year falls on February 6, 2027, and factories in China slow down in the weeks before it. For Ramadan gift boxes, calendar boxes and bags produced in China, the real deadline is the supplier's last pickup date in January, not the start of Ramadan.

With our lead times — about 7 working days for samples and 25–35 working days for bulk production after approval — artwork for a mid-January ship date should be final by mid-November 2026.

Boundary: Ramadan and Eid dates are expected dates that depend on moon sighting and may differ by a day between countries. Confirm the last pickup date and freight time with your supplier and forwarder.
