Adhesive engineering

The same label can fail on a different bottle.

Surface energy, curvature, texture, temperature, moisture, and application speed change how an adhesive wets out and holds. Tell us where the label will live—not only how it should look.

  • Permanent acrylic for stable glass, paperboard, and smooth plastics
  • High-tack options for textured, curved, or lower-energy surfaces
  • Removable and repositionable constructions where clean release matters
  • Cold-temperature and moisture-resistant options for refrigerated products

Plan condensation, ice-bucket, and adhesive testing for wine bottles

Finishing sequence

Build contrast in layers.

Start with legible print, then add one tactile or reflective focal treatment. The result is clearer, easier to produce, and often more premium.

Uncoated + foil

Natural paper balanced by a precise metallic mark.

Soft touch

Low-glare surface with foil or spot gloss contrast.

Foil + emboss

Metallic color registered to raised typography or detail.
