The engineering brief

The product decides more than the industry name.

Two skincare brands can need completely different packaging when one sells a single glass serum at retail and the other ships a five-piece subscription kit. We quote from the physical product and distribution route, not a generic industry template.

  • Product dimensions, weight, orientation, and fragile points
  • Retail shelf, gifting, ecommerce, or mixed-channel use
  • Manual or automated packing and label application
  • Destination, courier exposure, warehouse, and outer-carton needs

One packaging language

Match the box and label without pretending they print the same.

Different substrates and print methods shift color and sheen. A coordinated system uses shared references, then approves each component in its real material.

Reference

Define the visual target

Start with brand artwork, Pantone references where appropriate, and a physical master if an existing pack must be matched.

Translate

Adjust by substrate

Coated paper, uncoated stock, clear film, metallic material, and lamination influence the same ink differently.

Approve

Review real components

Approve boxes and labels under consistent light, with acceptable tolerances recorded before the bulk run.
