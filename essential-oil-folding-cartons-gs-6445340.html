What this reference shows

Read the visible evidence first.

The image is valuable for comparing a family of vertical packs and a consistent gold presentation. It does not show the internal fit, base lock, neck support, bottle clearance, or whether a carton can protect a particular glass container through a chosen distribution route.

01 · Architecture

Shared language across bottle sizes

Several tall cartons use the same color family and graphic rhythm. Each SKU still needs its own finished dimensions, dieline, copy panel, barcode zone, and packing orientation.

02 · Structure

Compact folding-carton route

The examples appear as upright paperboard cartons. Tuck direction, bottom style, board caliper, grain, glue, and any insert or neck support depend on the actual bottle and filled weight.

03 · Surface

Reflective gold visual direction

The broad gold field may be produced through different papers, inks, films, or foil treatments. Color, gloss, fold cracking, fingerprints, scuffing, and small-copy contrast need a material proof.

Configuration decisions

Confirm what the photograph cannot.

A project-ready quotation needs the physical product, artwork, production quantity, packing method, destination, and acceptance criteria. These decisions turn a useful image into a controlled specification.

Container data
Bottle width, height, shoulder and cap profile, filled weight, leakage risk, label, and required clearance.
Range plan
SKU count, shared and variable artwork, language versions, batch or date coding zones, and barcode placement.
Structure & finish
Board, tuck style, insert, gold method, print, finish, rub exposure, and packing-line sequence.
Compliance & supply
Required copy supplied by the buyer, target-market review, quantity by SKU, destination, sample stage, and target date.

Approval route

Test the real use before production.

Start with the production bottle and label. A white sample should check insertion, removal, cap clearance, bottom security, and panel bowing. The printed proof then confirms gold appearance, required copy, barcode quality, scores, and rub performance.

  • Measure the filled and labeled production container
  • Check fit and removal in a structural sample
  • Approve required text and barcodes at final size
  • Review gold surface and folds after realistic handling
