Privacy notice.

This notice explains how website inquiries, project specifications, and uploaded artwork are handled.

Information you provide

When you submit a quote form or contact us by email or WhatsApp, you may provide your name, work email, phone number, company or project information, product dimensions, quantity, delivery destination, target date, and reference artwork or images. A form submission may also include the page where the visit began, the page where the form was submitted, the referring page, and campaign parameters present in the link.

How the information is used

We use this information to understand your packaging request, ask technical questions, prepare pricing or samples, communicate about the project, maintain an order record if you proceed, and protect the website from spam or misuse. We do not use uploaded artwork to promote another customer’s project without permission.

Service providers and transfers

The website may use hosting, form-processing, email-delivery, and optional analytics providers. The current website setup uses Vercel hosting, Resend email delivery, and—only after analytics permission is granted—Google Analytics. If online form delivery is unavailable, the site may open your email application with a draft instead; your chosen email provider then handles that message. These services may process information in countries outside your own.

Retention

Inquiry and project information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, quote, manage an active or repeat project, resolve a dispute, or meet applicable record-keeping obligations. You may ask us to delete information that is no longer required, subject to any legal or contractual need to retain it.

Cookies and analytics

The site offers optional Google Analytics measurement using property tag G-LYNMPWG9WK. Analytics storage is denied by default and the Google tag is not loaded until you select “Allow analytics.” If allowed, Google Analytics may use cookies and collect page paths, approximate location derived from network information, browser and device information, referrer or campaign data, and website interactions such as contact clicks or a successful quote request. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, inquiry text, or uploaded file to Google Analytics, and advertising storage and ad personalization remain disabled in this implementation.

Your analytics choice is saved in local browser storage so the site can respect it on later visits. You can reopen the choice panel, decline future analytics collection, and remove the site’s accessible Google Analytics cookies at any time.

Separately, session storage may temporarily retain first-visit source information—including the landing page, referrer, campaign parameters, and a general discovery channel such as organic search, AI search, referral, or direct—so it can be included with a quote request. It is not sent through that workflow unless you submit the form and is cleared by the browser when the session ends.

Security and confidential files

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect submitted information, but no internet transmission is completely secure. Do not send highly sensitive information through a general quote form. If your project requires an NDA or a controlled file-transfer method, contact us before uploading confidential artwork.

Your choices

You may ask what contact or project information we hold, request a correction, or ask for deletion where applicable. Depending on where you live, local law may provide additional privacy rights.

Contact

For a privacy request, email kevin@GloryStarPack.com. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant inquiry, but do not send identity documents unless specifically required through a secure method.

This operational notice reflects the current website form workflow. It should be reviewed against the company’s legal entity, actual retention practices, target markets, and any analytics added before production launch.
