Structure comparison

Use the lightest structure that does the job well.

Start with presentation, protection, packing, storage, and freight. Decoration comes after the structure fits the commercial use.

Common custom packaging box families and the decision each one is best suited to solve.
Box familyBest suited toStorage / shippingKey decision
Rigid setup boxPremium retail, gifting, launch kits, and reusable presentationUsually ships assembled; collapsible variants may reduce volumeBoard, wrap, opening style, and insert must work as one system
Magnetic boxHigh-impact opening moments and repeated useSet-up or project-dependent collapsible constructionClosure feel, hinge, magnet alignment, and lid gap
Collapsible rigid boxPremium presentation with flat inbound storageShips flat and is assembled during pack-outFold interfaces, corner retention, closure, and assembly sequence
Corrugated mailerEcommerce, subscription, PR kits, and direct shippingShips flat before packing; optimized for courier handlingFlute, packed weight, internal movement, and closure
Shipping cartonWholesale cases, warehouse handling, and global logisticsShips flat before case packingLoaded weight, stacking, tape, inner protection, and pallet pattern
Folding cartonRetail units, secondary cosmetic boxes, and higher-volume packsShips flat and uses less storage spacePaperboard caliper, closure, crease behavior, and line packing

Internal engineering

The insert is where protection becomes presentation.

We size the cavity around the product, opening direction, accessory layout, shipping risk, and material preference. The right insert holds without making the customer fight the box.

  • Die-cut paperboard for recyclable, low-profile support
  • Molded pulp for shaped protection with a natural surface
  • EVA or EPE foam for impact resistance and exact cavities
  • Fabric-wrapped platforms for jewelry, watches, and gifting

Material decisions

Choose by performance, then by touch.

We recommend substrate and wrap combinations based on box structure, print method, finish, budget, and recycling goal.

Reliable color, foil, and lamination for polished retail packaging and precise brand matching.

Core structure
