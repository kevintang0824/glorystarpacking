Custom PR boxes for influencer kits and product launches

Choose the box, insert and shipping protection around what the recipient should see when the kit arrives.

Which packaging works for a PR box?

A PR box is a presentation pack sent to creators, editors or partners to introduce products or a launch. Use a stock box for a small pilot, a printed corrugated mailer for repeat parcel shipments, or a rigid presentation box with a protective outer shipper when the display box needs to arrive ready for filming. Test the complete package with the actual products before choosing.

A creator gifting discussion raised a useful planning question about sending gifts when coverage is uncertain. Etsy sellers comparing plain and branded boxes asked how branding fits around a shipping label. For a first wave, a stock shipper with the branding inside lets you test the unboxing while you confirm recipient count and kit contents.

Plan the opening sequence with a loaded sample

  1. Choose what the recipient sees first. Place the launch message where it is visible when the lid opens. Keep the main product name clear of fold lines and insert edges.
  2. Fit every product and accessory. Use filled containers and finished retail packs. Check cap clearance, finger access and whether cards, cables or small samples can slide out of position.
  3. Test the finished parcel. Pack, seal and label the sample as fulfillment will. Record outside dimensions and packed weight. For fragile items, review the carrier guidance and sample the protection for the intended route.
  4. Check what arrives. Photograph the opening and confirm the product stays in its cavity, the first message is visible, the surfaces are clean enough for the intended use and the customer can remove every item without damage.

Check the carrier's labeling and fragile-item guidance for the chosen service. UPS packaging and label guidance · FedEx guide to packing breakables.

PR box pack-out and quote checklist

Copy these fields into a brief for the brand, packaging supplier and fulfillment team.

  • Count the kits. Include the first wave, spare units, artwork versions, ship-to countries and the in-hand date.
  • List each SKU and accessory. Record dimensions with units, filled weight, fragile surfaces and required orientation.
  • Set the presentation requirements. Record the opening sequence, first visible message, insert material, removal access and acceptable marks after delivery.
  • Specify the finished parcel. Record whether it uses a ship-alone mailer or outer carton, the packed dimensions and weight, the label panel, cushioning and intended carrier service.
  • Log the sample decision. Keep the loaded sample revision, approval owner, photos after transport and the name of the person responsible for packing each shipment.

Ask suppliers to quote the carton, print, inserts, samples, packing labor and freight scope on comparable terms. For example, a 50-recipient pilot could use a stock mailer with a launch card and a tested insert. GloryStarPack's published minimum for custom paper boxes is 1,000 pieces per design. PR box questions

A PR box is a presentation pack sent to creators, editors or partners to introduce products or a launch. It usually holds the products, a short information card and any insert needed to keep the contents in place.
A corrugated PR mailer can ship on its own if the packed sample meets the carrier requirements and the agreed arrival standard. Use a protective outer shipper when the presentation box needs to remain clean for filming or when the contents need extra protection.
Start by comparing stock boxes with a branded card, sleeve or tissue. Ask for samples and a separate quote if you need a made-to-order structure. GloryStarPack publishes a minimum order of 1,000 pieces per design for custom paper boxes.

Build the PR kit around the packed products

Share the product dimensions, planned kit count and launch date when requesting a quote. Send the opening layout with the brief if different products require their own cavity or support.
